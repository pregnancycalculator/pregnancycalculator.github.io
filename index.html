How it works

Enter the first day of your last menstrual period. The calculator adds 280 days (40 weeks) to estimate your due date, the standard method used by clinicians. All results are estimates for information only and are not medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

How is a due date calculated?

Usually 280 days, or 40 weeks, from the first day of the last menstrual period. It is an estimate, and most births fall within two weeks either side.

How accurate is a due date?

It is a planning estimate. Only a small share of births happen on the due date itself, and ultrasound scans can adjust the date.

How many weeks pregnant am I?

Weeks are counted from the first day of the last period, not from conception. Use the weeks-pregnant tool with your last period date.

What are the trimesters?

The first trimester is weeks 1 to 13, the second is weeks 14 to 27, and the third is week 28 until birth.

Is this medical advice?

No. The calculators and guides are for general information only. Always follow the advice of your own clinician for your pregnancy.

When should I see a doctor?

As soon as you know you are pregnant, and promptly if you have heavy bleeding, severe pain, reduced movements late in pregnancy, or signs of labour before 37 weeks.
